* **What's on Screen**:
  1. Slowly pan across the national map from North India down to Coastal East and South.
  2. Hover over an **Uber H3 Hexagon cell** to demonstrate centroid density.
  3. Highlight the top 6 **KPI Metric Cards** (`Total Ingested: 53`, `38+ Active Stations`, `Pending Review: 4`, `92.5% AI Accuracy`).

> *"This is the **WeatherNexus National Operational Command Center**—running live on production right now.*

> *Right now, our algorithmic ranking places the **Wayanad Chooralmala Debris Flow** as Threat Number 1 with critical slope instability. Notice that low-threat systems—like the dissipated Remnant ARNAB in the Bay of Bengal—are automatically de-listed from active alarms once MHSI drops below 20.*

### [1:30 – 2:08] Section 4: Tri-Check AI Verification & The Radar Disproof
* **What's on Screen**:
  1. Click **"Weather Events Registry"** on the sidebar.
  2. Click the interactive **Filter Chips** (`Category: Rainfall`, `Severity: High`) to show instant multi-criteria filtering.
  3. Click on an incident row to open the **AI Evidence Ledger Modal**.
  4. Point your cursor sequentially across the 4 verification progress bars.
* **Delivery**: Emphasize the punchline; speak with conviction.

> *"Now for our core breakthrough: the **Tri-Check AI Verification Engine**.*
>
> *In our Weather Registry, officers can instantly filter records by date, location, or active severity chips. When we open any event, our AI Evidence Ledger doesn't guess—it provides a 4-tier auditable proof:*
>
> 1. *First, our **Multilingual Indic NLP Engine** analyzes syntax for panic keywords and clickbait markers.*
> 2. *Second, our **Computer Vision Model** compares uploaded ground photos against satellite optical passes to detect recycled imagery.*
> 3. *And third, our **Radar Ground-Truth Cross-Check** queries the nearest of India's 38 Doppler radar stations.*
>
> ***If a viral tweet claims a devastating cloudburst in Patna, but our Doppler radar recorded zero millimetres of rainfall—the report is instantly flagged, debunked, and quarantined.***
>
> *The resulting 0 to 100% TrustScore weights ground credibility at 55% and physical sensor data at 45%."*

### [2:08 – 2:42] Section 5: Citizen PWA, Rate Quota & Grievance Desk
* **What's on Screen**:
  1. Close modal. Click **"Citizen Field Report"** on the sidebar.
  2. Point to the live GPS detection badge and the photo upload dropzone.
  3. Click **"Grievance Redressal"** on the sidebar to display the dispute portal.
* **Delivery**: Empathetic, highlighting accountability.

> *"WeatherNexus connects the citizen directly with national disaster authorities.*

> *Our **Citizen PWA** lets ground observers submit geotagged incident reports with real-time GPS locking. To prevent coordinated bot attacks and panic spam during crises, we enforce a strict **hardware device quota of maximum 2 reports per physical device**.*
>
> *And if a genuine citizen observation was mistakenly down-ranked by AI? They can open the **Grievance Desk** to challenge the triage with mandatory live photographic evidence—ensuring 100% administrative accountability."*

### [2:42 – 3:12] Section 6: Authority Mode, Keyboard Hotkeys & Hindi Switch

  4. Press keyboard hotkey **`V`** to Verify or **`R`** to Reject!
  5. Click the **`HI` / `हिन्दी`** toggle in the top navbar. Pause 2 seconds as the UI translates.
* **Delivery**: Energetic and punchy.

> *"Now, let's step into official authority shoes. Docked securely at the bottom of our sidebar is the **Restricted Authority Area**.*
>
> *Once authenticated, the duty officer enters the **Human-in-the-Loop Review Desk**. Notice that our queue is strictly synchronized: exactly 4 pending alerts awaiting triage.*
>
> *During high-speed crisis management, every second counts. We built **Power Operator Hotkeys**: pressing **'V'** verifies an incident with Doppler corroboration; pressing **'R'** immediately rejects false alarms.*
>
> *Finally, disaster communication has no room for language barriers.*
>
> *(Click Hindi Toggle)*
>
> *With one click, our entire platform translates instantly into **Hindi**, ensuring field panchayats and state emergency operators receive synchronized, native intelligence."*

* **What's on Screen**:
  1. Click **"Overview" / "राष्ट्रीय स्थिति"** to return to the National Map.
